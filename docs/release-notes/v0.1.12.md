锁屏解锁后黑屏的进一步修复：显示器节能熄灭（DPMS）时自动唤醒、抓屏流停滞自动重建。/ Follow-up fixes for the black screen after unlock: wake DPMS-off outputs and rebuild stalled capture streams.

### 修复 / Fixed
- **解锁后/熄屏后黑屏（第二层原因）**：KDE 设置了"15 分钟关闭显示器"时，熄屏状态下所有 PipeWire 抓屏流都只发
  黑帧，且不会报任何错误。现在 portal 后端在连接建立、推流中每 30 秒、以及解锁时，都会通过
  `org.freedesktop.ScreenSaver.SimulateUserActivity` 重置会话空闲计时（相当于 X11 路径的虚拟鼠标微动），
  熄灭的显示器会被唤醒；新建抓屏流若 10 秒内没有产出任何帧（解锁后流静默停滞的情况），也会自动重建会话。
  同时新增帧流日志（`Portal stream delivering frames` / `rebuilt: reason=stalled`）便于诊断。
  Black screen after unlock or display power-off: DPMS-off outputs make every PipeWire stream deliver
  black frames without any error. The portal backend now calls SimulateUserActivity on connect, every 30 s
  while streaming and on unlock (mirroring the X11 fake-motion wake), and a stream that produces no frame
  within 10 s is rebuilt automatically. New frame-flow logs make this diagnosable.
